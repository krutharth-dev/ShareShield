# Intellectual property and licensing notes

> Planning notes only; not a legal opinion or a license grant.

## Repository status
This repository is private. **No software LICENSE file is being added yet.** In the absence of a license, do not assume general rights to copy, modify or redistribute code.

## Before selecting a software license
1. Establish who owns each contribution, and obtain any necessary permissions or assignments.
2. Check Malnad College of Engineering and applicable institutional IP policies.
3. Clarify roles and rights of all contributors, including people named in earlier project presentations.
4. Record third-party dependency licenses, notices and distribution duties.
5. Get legal advice before a patent filing, public disclosure, open sourcing, or commercial distribution.

## Dependency warning
PyMuPDF is offered under AGPL and commercial licensing terms. Private repository visibility does **not** remove obligations from third-party licenses. Verify actual distribution and service-use obligations, and consider a commercial license or suitable alternative if needed.

## Patent/research considerations
The project's proposed post-export leak verification is a **research direction**, not a confirmed new invention. Search prior art, maintain dated experimental records, and seek professional patent advice before disclosing any potentially patentable new technical details publicly. A college presentation may be relevant disclosure.

## Later decision
After ownership and dependency review, select and add the appropriate LICENSE file and attribution notices. Do not add a generic MIT/Apache license as a placeholder.
