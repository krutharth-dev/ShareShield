# ShareShield Code of Conduct

ShareShield welcomes constructive collaboration from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Our standards

Participants are expected to:
- Communicate respectfully, give actionable feedback, and assume good intent.
- Respect differences in experience, identity, and perspective.
- Keep discussions focused on the project, evidence, and user safety.
- Protect the privacy of users, contributors, and people depicted in documents.
- Accept corrections and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately sharing another person's private information are not acceptable. These standards apply in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and related project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness misconduct, contact the repository maintainer **privately** using a contact method offered on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/krutharth-dev). Do not publish identifying details or private communications in a public issue. For abuse occurring on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's built-in abuse-reporting mechanisms.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, ask contributors to change their behavior, restrict participation, or block access, depending on the circumstances. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed beyond the controls of the platform.

For software vulnerabilities rather than community conduct, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
